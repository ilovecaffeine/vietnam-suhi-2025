# Final Results

This directory contains the final datasets and analysis outputs generated by the SUHI workflow.

---

## Files

### `suhi_results.xlsx`
Excel workbook containing descriptive statistics, bootstrap results, spatial autocorrelation analysis (Global Moran's I and LISA), and urban–rural contribution analysis.

### `urban_id_table.csv`
Table listing all urban clusters with their unique IDs, pixel counts, and mapped areas.

### `urban_zones_suhi.geojson`
Urban polygons with calculated Surface Urban Heat Island (SUHI) attributes, including urban mean LST, rural mean LST, SUHI intensity, and pixel counts.

### `urban_zones_lisa.geojson`
Urban polygons with Local Moran's I (LISA) statistics and spatial cluster classifications (High–High, Low–Low, High–Low, Low–High, and Not Significant).
